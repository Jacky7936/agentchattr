# Design Context

## Target Audience

agentchattr is for developers and team leads coordinating several local CLI agents, such as Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Copilot CLI, Kimi, Qwen, Kilo, CodeBuddy, and MiniMax. Users are often switching between code, terminals, and the browser while they monitor agent work.

## Use Cases

- Keep humans and multiple agents in the same shared workspace.
- Route work with mentions, channels, jobs, rules, sessions, and threaded follow-up.
- Quickly understand who is active, what changed, what is blocked, and what needs a human decision.
- Review files, notifications, search results, member details, and conversation context without leaving the local app.

## Brand Personality / Tone

The interface should feel like an AI collaboration workbench: efficient, trustworthy, professional, and quietly opinionated. It should keep high information density without becoming terminal-like or generic. Mobile is a first-class surface, using drawers, sheets, compact controls, and resilient text wrapping instead of hiding core features.
